What Is This Website About?

Welcome to Innovate! This website explores the importance of Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure, the 9th UN SDG (United Nations Sustainable Development Goal). Through interactive features, interesting facts and simple lessons, this website is used to show how innovation, infrastructure and the industry can shape a better future for everyone.

What Are the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals?

The 17 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a collection of global goals established by the United Nations in 2015. They aim to address major challenges around the world. Those include: poverty, hunger, climate change, inequality and access to education. These goals provide a shared plan for countries to work towards a more peaceful, fair and sustainable world by 2030.

Why Does This Matter?

The Sustainable Development Goals matter because the challenges they address affect everyone. By working towards these goals, we can help protect the environment, improve people's quality of life and create better opportunities for future generations. Everyone has a part to play, whether through making responsible choices, supporting new ideas or helping their communities.

Why was this website created?

This website is created to inspire people to think creatively, understand the importance of sustainable development and recognise how new ideas can help build a better future. By spreading knowledge and encouraging innovative thinking, this website supports the goal of creating stronger industries, sustainable infrastructure and technological progress for the benefit of the world.
